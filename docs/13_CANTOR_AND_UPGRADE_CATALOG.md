# 13 — Cantor and Upgrade Catalog

**Status:** canonical content design; numerical tuning remains prototype-owned  
**Related:** Issue #14, `docs/11_QUEST_AND_COMPLETION_LEDGER.md`

Stillring's progression is built around **verbs**. Major verbs come from the critical path. Optional content expands their usefulness, convenience, and combinations without making the main route feel like a stripped-down demo.

---

# 1. What a Cantor Pattern is

A Cantor Pattern is an **operational tuning procedure** stored by the Cantor Key.

It is not a song the player must reproduce from memory and it is not a disguised keycard melody.

A pattern consists of a small set of resonant relationships—hold, offset, release, counterphase, drift tolerance—which the Key can apply to compatible authored machinery, seams, or diagnostic targets.

## Input / presentation rule

Default implementation target:

1. player focuses a compatible target or local field;
2. opens the Cantor wheel / quick pattern selector;
3. chooses a known pattern;
4. performs a short context-sensitive tuning interaction where timing or directional adjustment may matter;
5. receives strong visual, haptic, and audio feedback.

The game should not require memorizing copyrighted-style note strings or pausing to type a secret sequence.

Patterns must remain useful after their acquisition scene. Every shipping pattern below has at least three authored external uses.

# 2. Cantor Patterns — 9

## P01 — Common Measure
**ID:** `pattern.common_measure`  
**Acquisition:** Old Viaduct Refuge during the First Circuit  
**Teacher:** independent bellwright Eda Vell  
**Mandatory?** No for the first three dungeons; effectively core to many optional systems and strongly surfaced before Grand Ring.

### Function
Temporarily establishes a **local shared reference** between two or three nearby compatible civic machines without connecting them to Meridian phase.

It does not make them identical. It lets them agree on one practical operation for a short period.

### Authored uses
- repair Dori Senn's road-lantern mechanism in C04;
- stabilize a small Rootmere irrigation pair while preserving seasonal pressure differences;
- make two Saltreach manual fog indicators share a warning interval;
- after Stillness, create short moving pockets in local machines that were built to accept non-central references;
- optional community disconnection projects use it as the simplest proof that local coordination is possible.

### Design purpose
Plants the ending's philosophy mechanically before the player knows it is important.

---

## P02 — Hollow Measure
**ID:** `pattern.hollow_measure`  
**Acquisition:** Rootmere contract C08, after the player records both healthy irrigation tones  
**Mandatory?** No.

### Function
Sends a low-energy diagnostic pulse through a structure and displays **where resonance fails to return**, useful for locating voids, cavities, delaminated braces, or hidden service spaces.

It does not reveal treasure icons through walls.

### Authored uses
- identify the Mill Tailbox micro-vault in Brindle;
- distinguish a hollow diseased root from a root containing an old metal brace in Rootmere;
- find a Saltreach ballast cavity that sounds solid when flooded;
- locate a hidden Emberstep mold-cooling void;
- detect one Null Meridian worker bypass whose ceremonial cladding hides ordinary service architecture.

### Completion relationship
Several micro-vault clues become much more legible after Hollow Measure, but none rely on pixel hunting before acquisition.

---

## P03 — Breath Ledger
**ID:** `pattern.breath_ledger`  
**Acquisition:** Saltreach Lowwater Locker, after Tide Foundry  
**Mandatory?** No.

### Function
Records a short **pressure history** and replays it as a readable waveform on the Cantor Key.

The pattern does not create pressure; Glasslung Reed still performs the physical manipulation.

### Authored uses
- diagnose why a harbor gate fails only after three tide cycles;
- solve the optional Saltreach pressure trial by reading buildup rather than guessing valve order;
- reveal that an Emberstep steam line is failing upstream of the visible leak;
- compare pre/post-Stillness pressure behavior in the Leviathan inlet;
- identify a Null Meridian chamber that is preserving an earlier pressure state.

### Design purpose
Separates **diagnosis** from **physical action**, reinforcing Neris as a repairer rather than a spellcaster.

---

## P04 — Temper Cycle
**ID:** `pattern.temper_cycle`  
**Acquisition:** Emberstep Cold Kiln contract C15  
**Mandatory?** No.

### Function
Tracks and maintains a deliberately **non-uniform thermal cycle** across compatible metalwork.

Temper Gauntlet supplies heat transfer; the pattern tells the player when a piece should be allowed to expand, rest, or quench.

### Authored uses
- keep the Cold Kiln's useful uneven zones instead of flattening them;
- repair Rootmere resin/metal bracing without cooking living roots;
- restore a Saltreach boiler shell whose original alloy cannot survive state-standard heating;
- open an Emberstep erased-name vault by reproducing its maker's old heat treatment;
- one Null Meridian trial combines Temper Cycle with pressure history from Breath Ledger.

---

## P05 — Roadhand Pulse
**ID:** `pattern.roadhand_pulse`  
**Acquisition:** Tessa's Line Skiff tutorial after Grand Ring  
**Mandatory?** Yes for Line Skiff use.

### Function
Briefly wakes or identifies old **route reinforcement strips** without asking a central route bell for permission.

### Authored uses
- start and park the Line Skiff at compatible route points;
- reveal split junctions usable after the Switchshoe upgrade;
- expose dormant maintenance lanes during the moving-fault hunt;
- coordinate Tessa's independent courier relays in the Unringing;
- produce a final approach route toward Cairnspire using local route infrastructure.

### Rule
It never summons a vehicle from anywhere or turns every road into a fast-travel rail.

---

## P06 — Crosswind Measure
**ID:** `pattern.crosswind_measure`  
**Acquisition:** High Aerie Chainhouse Seven after restoring the first local airflow machinery  
**Mandatory?** Required within the High Aerie campaign after acquisition.

### Function
Measures disagreement between **air movement and suspended-structure motion**.

Vane Cloak manipulates the player's relationship to airflow; Crosswind Measure tells the player when a structure is lagging or leading the gust that appears to drive it.

### Authored uses
- High Aerie suspension machinery;
- C19 Chain Nine Sings;
- Line Skiff high-road mastery route where crosswind changes coasting distance;
- Cairnfall lenswalk alignment;
- final Anchor + Vane synthesis trial.

---

## P07 — Palinode Pattern
**ID:** `pattern.palinode`  
**Acquisition:** Bone Archive interlude  
**Mandatory?** Yes.

### Function
Allows two or more **incompatible resonance histories** to remain legible at the same time.

It does not tell the player which history is morally or factually “the true one.” It reveals where records/states disagree and what they share.

### Authored uses
- Bone Archive progression;
- C09 A Name Under Bark;
- High Aerie Varo history and C20;
- Mireglass Palace Under Reeds;
- C22 Witness at Reedwater;
- multiple late Resonance Faults;
- Quiet Court false diagnostic histories during Maelor's duel.

### Design purpose
A perception verb tied directly to Stillring's theme: contradiction can be preserved without forced resolution.

---

## P08 — Witness Interval
**ID:** `pattern.witness_interval`  
**Acquisition:** Mireglass, after learning Memory and completing the first optional post-dungeon testimony pair  
**Mandatory?** No.

### Function
Compares an object's current local resonance with **a recorded witness state supplied by the player**.

This is not a magical truth detector. It only answers: “How does this object differ from this specific recorded state?”

### Authored uses
- identify modifications made to Cairnspire public bells after their maker stamps were erased;
- compare a Rootmere living brace with Amsa's pre-Stillness journal sample;
- trace which Saltreach pressure chambers were added to the Leviathan in different decades;
- uncover a Cairnfall observation instrument secretly corrected by Meridian software;
- optional Quiet Court archive reconstruction.

### Completion role
Rewards players who collected testimonies by turning records into active tools instead of passive menu text.

---

## P09 — Local Accord
**ID:** `pattern.local_accord`  
**Acquisition:** Cairnfall C24 No Correction Required  
**Mandatory?** Optional for critical path; strongly relevant to community projects.

### Function
Negotiates a temporary shared operating window across **independent machines that retain their own local reference**.

Common Measure is small and immediate. Local Accord works at a community-infrastructure scale but requires the player to identify multiple acceptable tolerances first.

### Authored uses
- Rootmere distributed living resonators;
- Saltreach manual harbor timing;
- Emberstep clinic/foundry heat sharing;
- High Aerie archive/route signal coordination;
- Cairnfall independent observation network;
- optional Unringing preparedness bonuses before Null Meridian.

### Rule
Local Accord can fail when machines' safe ranges genuinely do not overlap. The answer is sometimes redesign, not stronger magic.

# 3. Tool Mastery Upgrades — 14

Upgrades change **possibility or handling**, not just percentages in a stat sheet.

## Anchor Line — 2

### A01 — Brake Reel
**ID:** `upgrade.anchor.brake_reel`  
**Source:** Rootmere Anchor handling trial  
**Effect:** allows Neris to stop a compatible moving/tensioned object at one intermediate authored catch point and rapidly reclaim the line after an interrupt.  
**New uses:** precision ferry-gate work, faster component-removal recovery in combat, several later suspended mechanisms.  
**Does not do:** freeform grappling/swinging.

### A02 — Split Cleat
**ID:** `upgrade.anchor.split_cleat`  
**Source:** C19 Chain Nine Sings  
**Effect:** allows two simultaneous **static brace points** on explicitly compatible structures. Neris cannot freely move while both are loaded.  
**New uses:** High Aerie chain repair, one Cairnfall starweight vault, final Anchor/Vane synthesis problem.

---

## Glasslung Reed — 2

### G01 — Metered Valve
**ID:** `upgrade.glasslung.metered_valve`  
**Source:** Saltreach pressure mastery trial  
**Effect:** choose a low-volume controlled pulse instead of the normal full discharge.  
**New uses:** delicate pressure locks, moving light objects without overshoot, exposing enemy vents without launching them away.

### G02 — Twin Chamber
**ID:** `upgrade.glasslung.twin_chamber`  
**Source:** post-Stillness C13 Two Captains, One Tide  
**Effect:** hold two **different authored pressure states** long enough to transfer them between nearby mechanisms.  
**Constraint:** not two generic ammo charges; only compatible intake sources can be stored.  
**New uses:** harbor coordination, late steam puzzles, Null Meridian pressure/temperature synthesis.

---

## Temper Gauntlet — 2

### T01 — Bimetal Palm
**ID:** `upgrade.temper.bimetal_palm`  
**Source:** C15 The Cold Kiln  
**Effect:** maintain two thermal zones across a held compatible object instead of forcing one average temperature.  
**New uses:** preserve flexible joints, repair mixed-alloy machinery, create controlled warp in select combat armor.

### T02 — Quench Collar
**ID:** `upgrade.temper.quench_collar`  
**Source:** Emberstep thermal mastery trial  
**Effect:** focuses Glasslung-assisted quench into a small ring around a target surface.  
**New uses:** crack sacrificial casings without destroying contents, interrupt one elite enemy armor cycle, open late micro-vault housings.

---

## Vane Cloak — 2

### V01 — Crossstay
**ID:** `upgrade.vane.crossstay`  
**Source:** High Aerie flight-path mastery trial  
**Effect:** one strong lateral correction while inside a valid authored airflow rather than only drifting with it.  
**New uses:** alternate vertical shortcuts, moving combat platforms, Cairnfall lenswalk.

### V02 — Storm Stitching
**ID:** `upgrade.vane.storm_stitching`  
**Source:** Storm Blind refuge service  
**Effect:** lets the cloak catch short **intermittent/dirty gusts** that would collapse a normal Vane deployment.  
**New uses:** late High Aerie secrets, one moving-fault hunt target, Null Meridian unstable-air route.

---

## Mirror Nail — 2

### M01 — Witness Etch
**ID:** `upgrade.mirror.witness_etch`  
**Source:** C22 Witness at Reedwater  
**Effect:** when pinning an echo-state, the Key can overlay the specific testimony/record that made that state legible.  
**New uses:** late investigative contracts, elite enemy tell identification, optional Court archive reconstruction.

### M02 — Duplex Head
**ID:** `upgrade.mirror.duplex_head`  
**Source:** Mireglass reflection mastery trial  
**Effect:** on specially authored **paired nodes**, one Nail can temporarily keep two linked local states committed together.  
**Constraint:** does not allow two arbitrary pins anywhere.  
**New uses:** Mireglass late vault, Cairnfall correction mechanism, final Mirror/seam synthesis.

---

## Cantor Key — 3

### K01 — Field Memory
**ID:** `upgrade.cantor.field_memory`  
**Source:** 6 Resonance Fault threshold  
**Effect:** stores the last unresolved/reconciled Fault's signature and can tell the player when a newly heard anomaly belongs to the same broad family.  
**Purpose:** teaches collection language without turning the Key into a treasure radar.

### K02 — Fine Fork
**ID:** `upgrade.cantor.fine_fork`  
**Source:** 30 Resonance Fault threshold  
**Effect:** better separation of close resonance layers, shortening recovery after complex diagnostic procedures and revealing certain micro-fault interactions.  
**New uses:** late Faults, moving-hunt tiers, one mastery-complex room.

### K03 — Seam Brace
**ID:** `upgrade.cantor.seam_brace`  
**Source:** Cairnfall Null Observation Pocket + C24 completion  
**Effect:** at compatible seam nodes, Neris can safely close the seam **after crossing** and redirect the residual pulse into a nearby authored mechanism.  
**New uses:** late Hush pockets, Still-Cairn optional routes, final seam-release trial.

## Line Skiff — 1

### L01 — Switchshoe
**ID:** `upgrade.lineskiff.switchshoe`  
**Source:** Line Skiff route mastery trial  
**Effect:** at marked legacy junctions, the Skiff can transfer between adjacent reinforcement strips without stopping.  
**New uses:** alternate courier routes, advanced moving-fault hunt targets, faster nonmandatory cross-region traversal.  
**Constraint:** does not let the Skiff leave supported road infrastructure.

# 4. Upgrade source policy

Optional upgrade sources are intentionally mixed:

- mastery trials reward demonstrated skill;
- repair contracts reward understanding a system/person;
- Fault thresholds reward broad exploration;
- refuges reward finding specialist infrastructure;
- micro-vaults provide components but generally do not hand out a finished capability with no context.

No major critical-path verb is sold for currency.

# 6. Vertical-slice requirement

The vertical slice does **not** need all nine patterns or fourteen upgrades.

It must prove the architecture can support:

- one optional Cantor Pattern learned from a person, not a dungeon chest;
- at least three uses for that pattern;
- one mastery upgrade that changes how a major tool is operated;
- one old-space revisit unlocked by the upgrade;
- persistence for learned patterns/upgrades using stable IDs;
- journal language explaining the new capability without a dense spreadsheet UI.

If that miniature loop is not fun, scaling it to twenty-three optional capability entries will only manufacture debt.
