**Status:** canonical content design; numerical tuning remains prototype-owned  
**Related:** Issue #14, `docs/11_QUEST_AND_COMPLETION_LEDGER.md`, `docs/104_COMPLETION_LEDGER_RECONCILIATION_AUTHORITY_ADDENDUM.md`

This catalog remains governed by the authored pattern/upgrade definitions already locked for Stillring. Completion Decision #16 reconciles the completion-sensitive thresholds below without changing the qualitative function of any pattern or upgrade.

## Canonical completion-sensitive authority

- Cantor Patterns: **9**.
- Tool Mastery Upgrades: **14**.
- `upgrade.cantor.field_memory` / K01 Field Memory: unlocks at **6 reconciled Resonance Faults**.
- `upgrade.cantor.fine_fork` / K02 Fine Fork: unlocks at **24 reconciled Resonance Faults**.
- No implementation may retain the superseded `30/48` Fault logic.

## K01 — Field Memory
**ID:** `upgrade.cantor.field_memory`  
**Source:** 6 Resonance Fault threshold  
**Effect:** stores the last unresolved/reconciled Fault's signature and can tell the player when a newly heard anomaly belongs to the same broad family.  
**Purpose:** teaches collection language without turning the Key into a treasure radar.

## K02 — Fine Fork
**ID:** `upgrade.cantor.fine_fork`  
**Source:** 24 Resonance Fault threshold  
**Effect:** better separation of close resonance layers, shortening recovery after complex diagnostic procedures and revealing certain micro-fault interactions.  
**New uses:** late Faults, moving-hunt tiers, one mastery-complex room.

## Completion Ledger integration

The Completion Ledger reads learned Pattern and Upgrade semantic IDs from progression authority; it does not own those facts. Each of the nine Patterns and fourteen Upgrades is one completion-bearing item inside its respective 6.25% bucket. Unlock source relationships do not create bonus completion IDs.

All authored functions, acquisition scenes, revisit responsibilities, and vertical-slice requirements from the prior catalog remain canonical unless they conflict with the explicit Decision #16 reconciliation above. This cleanup exists only to remove stale threshold authority and make the completion boundary unambiguous.
