## Purpose

The canonical `docs/story/07_SCENE_BEAT_LEDGER.md` predates the owner-approved Null Meridian Issue #11 decisions and still contains an older compact Movement 16 ordering in which:

`Independent Signals -> Paired Mechanic Trials -> Quiet Court Archive -> Maelor`

# Mapping to older scene-ledger labels

The following old labels remain useful as broad story beats but are no longer sufficient as exact adjacency authority.

## `M16-S03 — Paired Mechanic Trials`

This old umbrella now expands into the approved six-sequence Null Meridian structure rather than one undifferentiated block before Quiet Court.

Its content is distributed across:
- S1 Hanging Walk;
- S2 Equal House;
- S3 The Door That Was There;
- S4 Corrected Passage;
- S5 Battle That Won't End;
- S6 Take It Off the Bell.

The name `Paired Mechanic Trials` is therefore **historical shorthand**, not the preferred production identity.

Retail presentation should not label the finale as six numbered trials.

## `M16-S04 — Quiet Court Archive`

Still canonical as a mandatory evidence/reveal beat.

New precedence:

> Quiet Court is the **midpoint turn** of Null Meridian, not the immediate pre-boss scene.

After Quiet Court:
- Maelor begins deliberate correction/opposition indirectly through world behavior;
- S4 and S5 escalate traversal/combat/history pressure;
- a quiet Maelor personal-workspace beat follows;
- S6 mechanically proves local continuation after central relay disconnection;
- only then does the Maelor confrontation begin.

## `M16-S05 — Maelor / First Phase`

Still canonical.

It now occurs after S6 Take It Off the Bell.

## `M16-S06 — Maelor / Second Phase`

Still canonical.

No ordering change relative to Maelor Phase One.

## `M16-S07 — He Opens the Prison`

Still canonical.

Occurs after Maelor's defeat and leads into Movement 17 / Listener.

# Why precedence changed

The approved Issue #11 design establishes a stronger dungeon arc:

1. **physical coercion** — unlike structures forced into one motion;
2. **operational coercion** — unlike machines forced into one condition;
3. **historical correction** — official history erases worker reality;
4. **active correction** — Maelor forces route expressions toward one authorized version;
5. **preservation without ending** — old battle remains on top of the present;
6. **central disconnection proof** — local services continue after master relay stops;
7. **Maelor confrontation** — practical dependency argument has already been challenged through play;
8. **Listener** — the final cost of preventing endings becomes playable.

Putting Quiet Court immediately before Maelor would remove the approved second-half escalation and invalidate Decisions #3–#5.

Therefore newer authority controls.
