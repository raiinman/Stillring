# 1. Purpose

The regional manifest answers **where production happens**.

This companion answers four harder questions:

1. what are the major authored dungeon/pressure complexes;
2. what encounter families belong where;
3. how much Line Skiff network content must the world support;
4. what makes Null Meridian uniquely expensive without turning it into an unrelated second game.

Core rule:

> **A dungeon is a concentrated argument made through existing world language, not a themed box that introduces arbitrary mechanics.**

And for traversal:

> **The Line Skiff network is world infrastructure first and optional-content scaffolding second.**

---

# 2. Production-complex classes

## D0 — regional critical-path complex

Major authored space whose mechanics, art, narrative, audio and encounter language define a region.

## D1 — late return / accumulated-verb complex

Uses remembered geography or prior regional language but becomes accessible/meaningful through later verbs.

## D2 — state-pressure complex

Encounter burden is distributed through world-state manipulation rather than a conventional room/boss sequence.

## D3 — mastery complex

Optional authored content built to prove system mastery without becoming a second campaign.

## D4 — finale synthesis complex

Null Meridian. Recombines prior systems under one governing idea and carries unique narrative/finale burden.

# 3. Dungeon/complex minimum production packet

Every major complex must eventually have:

- semantic production ID;
- canonical story purpose;
- region / world-state context;
- entry motivation;
- ordinary-world ancestry where applicable;
- central mechanical relationship;
- required prior verbs;
- new acquisition/procedure if any;
- major encounter/pressure package;
- narrative evidence/reveal;
- art kit + hero assets;
- Hush relationship class;
- animation families;
- audio/music states;
- VFX/readability states;
- UI/objective/journal burden;
- save/checkpoint boundaries;
- optional/revisit content relationship;
- reuse-versus-unique classification;
- System IDE fixtures;
- future runtime/human-play questions.

No complex is production-ready if its brief is only “forest dungeon,” “water dungeon,” or “final dungeon.”

# 4. Root Cathedral — D0 / Rootmere

## Narrative purpose

Prove that “repair” can mean removing harmful imposed structure rather than restoring an official configuration.

## Entry motivation

Neris needs a trustworthy Rootmere baseline and follows evidence that the bell-oak is not the source of the fault.

## Core mechanical relationships

- living load paths;
- water routing;
- tension;
- structural support versus immobilization;
- first Anchor Line production teaching.

## Required content

- approach through lived Rootmere infrastructure;
- diagnosis route from surface symptoms to buried braces;
- Anchor Line reconstruction from maintenance parts;
- Mossjaw pressure encounter;
- Mawhart boss/liberation encounter;
- component-removal/load-path readability;
- Ilyra warning plate/evidence;
- post-complex community response.

## Encounter package

- **Mossjaw — E0 ordinary fauna** with accidental maintenance harness;
- **Mawhart — E1 infrastructure-entangled fauna**; survives and retreats after liberation.

## Reuse / uniqueness

Reuse:
- Rootmere living/civic kit;
- ferry/water/brace language;
- common Anchor interaction grammar.

Unique:
- bell-oak hero structure;
- major foreign-brace relationship;
- Mawhart;
- dungeon-scale living-load compositions.

## Hush/state burden

Root Cathedral itself need not become a complete second Hush dungeon unless current story authority explicitly requires a paired area.

Later Rootmere/Hush content should reuse semantic place relationships selectively.

## Hard failures

- “nature temple” abstraction;
- creature-kill climax;
- arbitrary glowing weak points;
- dungeon geometry that implies universal climbing;
- visually healing Mawhart through magic purification.

# 13. Broken Standard Yard — D3 mastery complex

Completion authority treats Broken Standard Yard as:
- **1 global completion item**;
- built from **7 internal authored commissions**.

## Production rule

The seven commissions are real content workload but not seven global completion buckets.

The complex should:
- reuse accumulated tool/mechanism families;
- test synthesis and repair reasoning;
- provide clear retry/progress state;
- avoid becoming seven disconnected tutorial rooms;
- avoid a score treadmill as the only mastery proof.

Exact internal commissioning sequence and room decomposition follow its current mastery authority.

No new global completion items may be created from individual commissions.

# 14. Skill Trial / optional elite spatial policy

## Skill Trials — 9

Each trial must have a world/infrastructure reason to exist.

Required burden per trial:
- one mastery proposition;
- legitimate region/space;
- clear start/reset/finish state;
- relevant UI/audio feedback;
- no unrelated bespoke art package unless the trial genuinely earns it.

### Trial 05

Trial 05 is the Line Skiff mastery course.

It must remain an old courier/service loop with believable infrastructure, not a floating racing stadium.

## Optional Elite Encounters — 8

Each elite requires:
- unique semantic resolution ID;
- region-appropriate enemy/mechanism foundation;
- one meaningful authored pressure idea;
- legitimate world placement;
- save/reload state;
- completion settlement;
- proportionate production burden.

An elite does not need a unique skeleton, soundtrack and arena kit by default.

Reuse is expected where it preserves identity and readability.

# 19. Drift Knot production efficiency rule

Default:

> **Reuse believable ordinary/story/courier route infrastructure.**

A bespoke Hunt segment is justified only when its mechanical/fictive need cannot be expressed on a legitimate existing or legacy route.

Acceptable special reasons:
- abandoned maintenance bypass;
- observation loop;
- freight connector;
- emergency strip.

Unacceptable reason:
- “we needed somewhere to put Knot #8.”

The same corridor may legitimately serve:
- travel;
- courier/world-state use;
- story approach;
- Drift Knot;
- maintenance content;
- community project.

Multi-use is preferred but not forced as a quota.

# 22. Null Meridian — D4 finale synthesis package

Null Meridian is the project's largest unique content concentration.

It must nevertheless remain recognizably constructed from the same civic, maintenance, acoustic and human world the player has spent the game learning.

## Governing idea

The dungeon shows systems, structures and histories being forced toward one authorized condition, then proves through player action that local continuation can survive central disconnection.

It is not six arbitrary “ultimate trials.”
