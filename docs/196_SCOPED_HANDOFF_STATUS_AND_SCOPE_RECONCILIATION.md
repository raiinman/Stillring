# 7. Six major regions versus eight ordinary-world production families

The final blind audit found wording that could look contradictory if a producer does not know the project chronology.

The correct taxonomy is:

## Seven story/world roles at ordinary scale

### Opening / home production family

**Brindle / First Circuit / Graymile**

This is not an additional major adventure region.

It is the opening/home/circuit production family that establishes:
- Neris and ordinary bellwright work;
- regional-cultural postcards;
- roads/Graymile;
- the before-memory foundation;
- First Circuit content and later changed-world revisits.

It requires full environment/content production even though it is not counted among the six major adventure regions.

### Capital / hub production family

**Cairnspire**

This is the capital/hub and Grand Ring center.

It is not one of the six major regional adventure cultures.

It requires full production for:
- living civic baseline;
- institutional/hub content;
- Grand Ring;
- changed civic-island state;
- Hush-Cairnspire relation;
- Unringing/credits consequences.

### Six major adventure regions

1. **Rootmere**
2. **Saltreach**
3. **Emberstep**
4. **High Aerie**
5. **Mireglass**
6. **Cairnfall**

These are the six major regional cultures introduced through the First Circuit and developed as major adventure regions across the campaign.

## Production-family count

Environment/world production therefore has **eight ordinary-world production families**:

1. Brindle / First Circuit / Graymile;
2. Cairnspire;
3. Rootmere;
4. Saltreach;
5. Emberstep;
6. High Aerie;
7. Mireglass;
8. Cairnfall.

This does not mean the design scope changed from six major adventure regions to eight major adventure regions.

It means opening/home and capital/hub content require real production families too.

## Late transformed/finale burden

Hush-Cairnspire, Still-Cairn and Null Meridian are late transformation/finale production burdens derived substantially from established world language.

They are not counted as additional ordinary-world major regional cultures.
