# 1. Purpose

This document converts Stillring's regions from story descriptions into an environment/prop package an external art team can actually estimate.

The governing rule is:

> **Build the life of the place before building its ruins.**

A region is not production-ready if the asset list only contains:

- landscape;
- dungeon;
- landmark;
- catastrophe variant.

Every region also requires the ordinary things that prove people live there:

- work surfaces;
- food/service spaces;
- storage;
- maintenance tools;
- freight;
- warning systems;
- route infrastructure;
- civic records/signage;
- domestic repair;
- seating and gathering;
- local fallback systems;
- visible adaptations after Stillness.

The player must form a visual **before-memory** before changed-world art can matter.

# 3. Required state package per region

Every major region must account for:

- **S0 Ordinary Waking** — lived pre-crisis baseline;
- **S1 Local-resolution** — visible outcome of regional dungeon/contract work;
- **S2 Grand Ring / Stillness** — specific state consequences, not generic destruction;
- **S3 Changed-world operation** — emergency adaptation and moving pockets;
- **S4 Unringing** — local coordination system visibly taking shape;
- **S5 Credits/Epilogue** — continued life, repair and changed practice.

Hush pairing is handled where canon actually requires a paired representation. It is not a blanket second copy of every mesh.

---

# 4. Modular/environment rules

All regional kits must obey:

- large-form silhouette before texture variation;
- believable construction joints;
- predictable snapping logic later chosen by technical production;
- authored traversal clarity without implying universal climbability;
- collision intent planned during blockout;
- no hero vista built entirely from one repeated kit module;
- repair variants must correspond to how residents actually maintain the structure;
- state variants should reuse base geometry where honest rather than creating wasteful full copies;
- exact dimensions remain dependent on approved Neris/camera/traversal scale.

Environment art may not independently invent new traversal verbs, weak points, destructibility or puzzle semantics.

## 13.3 Reuse doctrine

Late-game power comes from recognition.

Prefer:
- familiar civic pieces in wrong relationships;
- known rooms with one preserved impossible assumption;
- repeated maintenance geometry;
- old service systems exposed.

Avoid:
- universal black void;
- generic cosmic architecture;
- glowing fantasy cathedral unrelated to Orra;
- random impossible geometry with no semantic correspondence.

# 16. C3 production minimum

Every C3 named recurring character enumerated in this document must receive, at minimum:

- named head/face solution;
- occupation-readable silhouette;
- assigned regional garment family;
- one signature work/prop relationship where appropriate;
- continuity notes across ordinary, changed-world and Unringing states;
- facial tier assignment;
- reuse class.

`C3+` means the character requires stronger bespoke treatment because she/he/they:

- owns a major contract;
- recurs across multiple critical states;
- anchors ordinary-life attachment;
- carries major regional institutional argument;
- or must remain immediately recognizable during catastrophe/credits.

C3+ does not automatically mean unique skeleton/body.

# 18. Asset-state reuse policy

Prefer parameter/material/prop-state changes when they honestly express the state.

Create new geometry when:
- topology/structure truly changed;
- traversal/route changed;
- repair added real structure;
- Hush correspondence requires altered spatial relationship;
- catastrophe created physically different support/load state.

Do not duplicate whole regional kits simply to produce:
- dirtier version;
- purple Hush version;
- darker version;
- “destroyed” version.
