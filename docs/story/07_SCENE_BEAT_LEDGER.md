# Project Stillring — Final Scene Beat Ledger

**Status:** final canonical critical-path production map  
**Related:** `docs/02_STORY_BIBLE.md`, `docs/story/06_CHARACTER_ARCS_AND_RELATIONSHIPS.md`, `docs/story/08_REVEAL_AND_FORESHADOW_LEDGER.md`, `docs/story/09_MAIN_QUEST_OBJECTIVE_FLOW.md`, `docs/story/10_DIALOGUE_ANCHORS.md`, `docs/12_100_PERCENT_ROUTE.md`

This is the authoritative mandatory story map. It supersedes earlier scene ordering and any red-team patch note that conflicts with it.

A scene may be gameplay, hybrid staging, walk-and-talk, or cinematic. It is not synonymous with “cutscene.”

## Control labels

- **PLAYABLE** — ordinary player control carries the story.
- **HYBRID** — player control with bounded dialogue/camera/state beats.
- **WALK/TALK** — constrained travel/work while characters converse.
- **CINEMATIC** — control intentionally removed.

## Production rules

1. If important exposition happens while nobody wants anything and the player does nothing, rewrite it.
2. Every destination must follow from what Neris currently wants, not merely from a quest marker.
3. A mechanic may be explained once. Later scenes should demonstrate, complicate, or assume it.
4. Quiet scenes and ordinary-life scenes are structural.
5. Every major normal-world region must be personally knowable before Grand Ring changes it.
6. Important characters continue acting while Neris is elsewhere.
7. Optional content may deepen a mandatory scene; it may not contain the only explanation required to understand it.

# MOVEMENT 0 — BRINDLE / FIRST RING

## M00-S01 — One. Listen.
**Control:** CINEMATIC → PLAYABLE  
**Place:** Vale bell shed  
**Participants:** child-memory Neris, Ilyra voice, present Neris

A childhood memory of Ilyra teaching Neris to hear a cracked cup cuts into present Neris waking under a workbench.

**Change:** Ilyra begins as sensory memory rather than biography; “listen again” becomes a family habit; imperfection is meaningful before it is thematic.

**Objective out:** help Orin finish First Ring repairs.

## M00-S02 — Morning Work
**Control:** PLAYABLE  
**Participants:** Neris, Orin, villagers

Carry parts, diagnose a mill signal, deliver a bracket, climb a roof route, and help prepare for First Ring. C01 Crooked Fire Bell may begin here.

Orin pockets a Cairnspire-marked letter when Ilyra is mentioned.

**Change:** Brindle is work, food, gossip, children, roofs, and ordinary civic infrastructure before it becomes a wound.

## M00-S03 — Wooden Blades
**Control:** PLAYABLE / tutorial spar  
**Participants:** Neris, Tessa

Tessa waits for Orin to repair a courier fitting and spars with Neris.

**Change:** combat basics arrive through existing chemistry; Tessa mentions worsening route-bell failures without framing them as apocalypse.

## M00-S04 — After the Ring
**Control:** WALK/TALK  
**Participants:** Neris, Orin

Orin starts opening Ilyra's old traveling case, hears festival bells, and stops.

> “After the Ring, we talk. Properly.”

**Change:** Orin truly intends to stop withholding. Eleven years of delay run out before intention becomes action.

## M00-S05 — The Bell That Does Not Ring
**Control:** HYBRID  
**Place:** Brindle relay square

The player moves through First Ring celebration while distant regional bells answer. Cairnspire should answer last.

It does not.

Animals go silent. Flame leans without sound. Rain hangs for a fraction of a second.

**Objective out:** get back to the workshop with Orin.

## M00-S06 — The Floor Compartment
**Control:** HYBRID / crisis

The Vale workshop destabilizes. Neris and Orin expose Ilyra's sealed compartment and the prototype Cantor Key.

Orin:

> “Your mother didn't die where they said she did.”

The Hush tears through the room and takes him before explanation.

## M00-S07 — First Sight of the Hush
**Control:** PLAYABLE → CINEMATIC

Neris strikes the Key during the breach, crosses into an impossible impression of Brindle for seconds, sees Orin looking back, and is thrown into the Waking World.

Unknown to Neris, the overload damages the Key and partially writes Neris's emergency local calibration into its field memory.

**Objective out:** reach Cairnspire, learn what happened, and find a road back to Orin.

**Control:** HYBRID / active confrontation

- he deliberately selected the three reference regions;
- he deliberately disabled one First Ring damping layer to force hidden system failure into the open;
- he expected a controlled crisis and did not target Orin;
- Meridian has already committed to the first entrainment step.

> “Ask her.”

Neris:

> “She's dead.”

Maelor:

> “That has always been the most convenient version.”

> “I was wrong to believe your mother would accept the cost.”

Neris:

> “What cost?”

> “Another tomorrow.”

No explanatory monologue is inserted between those lines.
