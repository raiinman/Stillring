# Project Stillring — World Pulse Ledger

**Status:** canonical living-world continuity contract  
**Related:** Issue #19, regional story bibles, `11_NARRATIVE_FLOW_AUDIT.md`

This ledger answers one question:

> What changes while Neris is somewhere else?

A living world cannot wait at quest-state zero until the protagonist returns.

Stillring's world-state changes must be legible through work location, notices, relationships, construction, shortages, celebrations, rumors, prices/services, local arguments, and who is physically present.

The Grand Ring creates literal Stillness. The narrative outside that mechanic must do the opposite.

---

# Global milestone pulses

## Pulse A — after Cairnspire registration / before Rootmere

- Brindle continues First Ring cleanup; Mara Pell either continues fire-bell repair or argues with Orin's absent workshop over parts.
- Cairnspire queues grow and some neighborhood repair crews begin working unofficially after hours.
- Tessa's courier contacts spread rumors that the capital is hiding the scale of the outage.
- High Aerie sends additional accident records toward Cairnspire.
- Saltreach merchants begin hoarding spare manual fog equipment.
- Emberstep inspectors receive the first state request for emergency bell-metal analysis.

## Pulse B — after Rootmere

- Amsa Wren publishes a correction blaming old braces rather than the bell-oak itself.
- Tavin Ro sends a cautious inspector note arguing that removal without replacement planning could create new risk.
- Cairnspire maintenance workers copy Ilyra's warning phrase onto unofficial chalkboards even though they do not know what it means.
- Rusk tightens information control, believing panic will cause incompatible repairs.
- Saltreach captains complain Rootmere got priority while their fog failures worsen.
- Eda Vell hears about Neris and leaves a sarcastic note at the Old Viaduct Refuge.

## Pulse C — after Saltreach

- Tessa's old manifest begins circulating through courier gossip before any formal investigation.
- Pellon Vey quietly checks whether other route records contain similar sealed shipments.
- Rusk opens an internal inquiry but refuses public release of unverified material.
- Sena Ash obtains a copy through courier channels and becomes more hostile to Cairnspire's impurity accusation.
- Vessa Mor starts repainting manual signal marks without waiting for authorization.
- Lio Fen hears a Saltreach story about a creature used as infrastructure and makes an uncomfortable comparison to Rootmere's bell-oak.

## Pulse D — after Emberstep / rush to Cairnspire

- regional contacts start moving copies of the three-point reference record independently;
- Rusk orders restricted Meridian access and believes he is preventing sabotage;
- route workers quietly prepare evacuation paths because Tessa has stopped trusting official reassurance;
- Caldrin leaves High Aerie carrying contradictory source records;
- Sena's workers begin stamping names back onto emergency castings before any law changes;
- public rumor incorrectly says Neris caused the network failures by using an illegal Key.

## Pulse E — after Grand Ring

World pulse becomes geographically irregular because Stillness controls who can act.

Every region gets:
- at least one moving pocket;
- at least one stranded group;
- one authority vacuum;
- one local practice that unexpectedly works;
- one old dependency that fails.

## Pulse F — after Motion

- Tessa's courier relief stops calling itself temporary in practice even if Tessa still does in dialogue.
- High Aerie copies begin traveling outward.
- some Cairnspire workers use conflicting copies to avoid repeating one bad repair.
- Saltreach captains start exchanging manual timing windows.
- Rootmere keepers begin publishing actual water/load conditions instead of waiting for central timing.

## Pulse G — after Palinode / before Mireglass resolution

- disputed histories become newly usable evidence in several old regions;
- some NPCs resent Neris for reopening arguments they considered settled;
- one Cairnspire notice board now shows two versions of the same accident report side by side;
- Olo Wren begins asking Caldrin questions through courier notes;
- Rusk, if mobile, objects to anonymous document release while admitting suppressed records can no longer remain sealed.

## Pulse H — after Memory / before Cairnfall

- Mirror-accessible echoes appear in old regions;
- some families request help seeing old states; others ask that seams be marked and left alone;
- Mireglass debate over civic seam use begins before the late community project;
- Ilyra becomes less controlling about her workbench arrangement;
- Neris/Tessa conversation shifts from "when this is over" to "what happens if the old route system never comes back?"

## Pulse I — after Cairnfall

- moving pockets last longer;
- every major region begins its own disconnection experiments without waiting for Neris;
- some experiments fail and require repair;
- first independent cross-region reports use compatible formats but different local timing;
- public confidence in a single restored Meridian solution collapses.

## Pulse J — after Ending / Unringing

- Orin returns to a world that has changed without him;
- Brindle starts rebuilding before the finale;
- Tessa's network has more volunteers than she has assignments;
- regional projects reach different maturity based on player involvement;
- Null Meridian route becomes a collective consequence, not a hero-only portal.

# Service/economy pulse rules

Services may change for in-world reasons:
- shortages;
- a worker moving;
- local repair completion;
- a route reopening;
- Stillness windows;
- community disconnection.

Avoid arbitrary RPG inflation.

Examples:
- Saltreach pressure parts become cheaper after dry dock work, but imported cloth becomes scarce.
- Emberstep repair services remain strong while food becomes expensive after route disruption.
- High Aerie Vane service improves after Sal's crews reopen Chainhouse Seven.
- Cairnspire shops may close while Fourth Landing barter/service activity expands.

---

# Rumor rule

Rumors are allowed to be wrong.

At each major milestone, children, workers, travelers, and officials should spread multiple interpretations.

Examples after Rootmere:
- “The kid from Brindle healed the bell-tree.”
- “The Bellwardens poisoned it.”
- “Nothing was wrong with the tree; the state just wanted an excuse to cut it.”
- “Maelor sent the kid there personally, so obviously the capital knew.”

The game does not immediately correct every rumor.

A living world includes bad information.

---

# Completion-state rule

Optional completion can accelerate, deepen, or personalize local changes.

It cannot be the reason all change exists.

A region with 0% optional completion still acts.

A 100% region acts with more preparation, more named people, more routes, and richer credits evidence.
