# Project Stillring — Emotional Pacing Map

**Status:** canonical pacing audit  
**Related:** Issue #19, `11_NARRATIVE_FLOW_AUDIT.md`

Stillring should not feel like thirty hours of escalating thematic seriousness.

The target waveform is closer to:

`comfort -> curiosity -> competence -> wonder -> suspicion -> relief -> humor -> pressure -> triumph -> unease -> betrayal -> grief -> rediscovery -> competence -> fracture -> wonder -> dread -> rebuilding -> resolve -> release`

The player needs emotional contrast for major moments to land.

---

# Pacing rules

1. No more than two major high-intensity sequences back-to-back without a quieter playable interval unless the story intentionally wants exhaustion.
2. A dungeon clear should not always be followed immediately by a revelation cutscene.
3. At least one major travel/return segment after each regional climax should contain ordinary life, humor, or relationship texture.
4. Exposition-heavy scenes should be separated by action or silence.
5. The player must experience joy/beauty in every major region before its hardest version.
6. Ilyra, Tessa, Orin, Maelor, Caldrin, Sena, and Rusk each need scenes where their function is not simply “deliver plot.”
7. After Grand Ring, hope must re-enter before the second half becomes emotionally exhausting.

---

# Movement waveform

## M00 — Brindle / First Ring

**Primary:** comfort, affection, curiosity.  
**Secondary:** mild family tension.  
**End:** shock/urgency.

### Required lightness
- small village work;
- Tessa sparring banter;
- Orin being irritatingly practical;
- children / festival preparation;
- mundane repair mistakes.

### Avoid
Foreshadowing every object as ominous. Brindle must feel genuinely normal.

---

## M01 — Road to Cairnspire

**Primary:** urgency easing into scale/wonder.  
**Secondary:** Tessa/Neris rapport.

The ridge view is the first breath after crisis.

The road should have one moment where the player simply sees Orra opening up.

---

## M02 — Cairnspire

**Primary:** curiosity, frustration, competence.  
**Secondary:** social comedy in bureaucracy.

Rusk confrontation provides tension; Route House and city work should also be funny and human.

Maelor's first private scene is quiet, not sinister-score villain foreshadowing.

---

## M03 — Expanded First Circuit

**Primary:** wonder, freedom, social attachment.  
**Secondary:** low-level mystery.

This is the largest pre-pivot breathing chapter.

Each regional postcard gets a different emotional color:
- Rootmere — warmth / tactile curiosity;
- Saltreach — bustle / humor;
- Emberstep — energy / craft pride;
- High Aerie — exhilaration / beauty;
- Mireglass — oddness / playfulness;
- Cairnfall — calm / awe.

The player should finish the circuit wanting to see more of Orra.

---

## M04 — Rootmere full crisis

**Primary:** friction -> competence -> relief.  
**Secondary:** first serious suspicion.

Mawhart resolution should feel like a win.

Do not immediately bury that triumph under a ten-minute conspiracy dump. Ilyra's warning is one sharp splinter.

### Post-region breath
Ferry / meal / repair / Maelor personal anecdote.

---

## M05 — Saltreach

**Primary:** adventure / uncertainty / rising personal guilt.  
**Secondary:** coastal freedom.

The harbor should remain fun enough that the player enjoys being there despite the anomaly.

Tessa confession lands after a mechanical victory and must be followed by quieter travel rather than another crisis immediately.

---

## M06 — Emberstep

**Primary:** social pressure / craft exhilaration / suspicion becoming proof.

The Kiln should be one of the most physically energetic sequences in Act One.

The archive discovery shifts mood sharply into urgency.

No optional-completion sweep is emotionally foregrounded after the three-point discovery.

---

## M07 — Grand Ring

**Primary:** dread -> betrayal -> frantic agency -> helplessness.

This is the first sustained peak.

The confrontation should be short enough that the playable failure remains the emotional centerpiece.

Grand Ring montage hurts because the circuit gave the player before-memories.

---

## M08 — Ilyra reunion

**Primary:** disbelief -> anger -> awkwardness.  
**Secondary:** fragile relief.

Do **not** make this another high-intensity lore peak.

After recognition, give the player:
- silence;
- workbench action;
- one failed attempt at ordinary mother/child familiarity;
- one small laugh or uncomfortable mundane correction.

Only one urgent technical next-step should leave the sequence.

---

## M09 — Changed Brindle / Line Skiff

**Primary:** grief -> recognition -> renewed motion.  
**Secondary:** friendship/humor.

Tessa and the seeded Skiff provide the game's major emotional injection of agency after catastrophe.

The first successful fast run should feel **fun**, not merely desperate.

This is important.

---

## M10 — High Aerie / Motion

**Primary:** danger -> exhilaration -> moral complexity -> release.

Because the player remembers normal wind, the post-Stillness motion has contrast.

After Motion, Wind Kitchen or Chainhouse scene should be warm and noisy before Bone Archive.

---

## M11 — Bone Archive

**Primary:** curiosity -> unease -> personal accusation.

Shorter, denser, stranger.

Do not make it visually or emotionally as huge as High Aerie.

End with Ilyra admission, but not full Mercy Window reconstruction.

### Required recovery beat
Travel or workbench scene afterward where Neris and Ilyra do not resolve the conflict.

---

## M12 — Mireglass / Memory

**Primary:** eerie beauty -> intimacy -> fracture.

The region must include ordinary funny/beautiful memory behavior before Palace intensity.

Full Mercy Window truth is the emotional low point between Neris and Ilyra.

Do not immediately start Cairnfall urgency in a cutscene.

Give the player one lonely/free-roam window first.

---

## M13 — Cairnfall

**Primary:** intellectual wonder -> competence -> cautious collaboration.

This chapter deliberately lowers interpersonal volume.

Neris and Ilyra rebuild trust through engineering together rather than a forgiveness speech.

Public astronomy / disagreement makes the location feel hopeful.

---

## M14 — Still-Cairn / Ending

**Primary:** temptation -> grief -> anger -> release.

This is another sustained peak, but its texture differs from Grand Ring.

The threat is not speed. It is comfort.

After Orin wakes, leave silence around the confession.

No immediate family hug.

---

## M15 — Unringing

**Primary:** rebuilding / agency / community pride.

This is the game's most important late breathing phase.

The player should see people solve problems without Neris.

Optional completion here feels like helping a living world, not delaying the final boss.

Include celebrations, meals, arguments, and visible repairs.

---

## M16 — Null Meridian / Maelor

**Primary:** dread -> mastery -> confrontation.

Final dungeon should feel like competence, not punishment.

The player knows the verbs.

Maelor confrontation is emotionally hard but mechanically readable.

His argument should be shorter than the world evidence supporting it.

---

## M17 — Listener / release

**Primary:** wonder -> temptation -> sorrow -> release.

Not a bigger louder boss.

Preserved alternatives should initially feel genuinely desirable.

The final destructive act should feel both correct and costly.

---

## M18 — Epilogue

**Primary:** tenderness / incompleteness / possibility.

The game slows down.

No urgent UI.

People are working.

Some arguments are unresolved.

The player gets to say goodbye by walking.

---

# Main relationship waveforms

## Neris / Tessa

`friendly rivalry -> practical partnership -> personal trust -> Saltreach rupture -> repaired friendship -> crisis co-leadership -> chosen future road`

Required non-plot scenes:
- early spar;
- camp food complaint;
- road-shortcut argument;
- one post-Rootmere joking repair;
- awkward post-Saltreach silence;
- first fun Skiff run;
- post-Memory conversation about what happens after;
- Unringing paperwork/route joke.

## Neris / Ilyra

`memory -> impossible reunion -> anger -> controlled truth -> accusation -> adult collaboration -> incomplete rebuilding`

Required non-plot scenes:
- Ilyra remembers Neris's childhood food preference incorrectly;
- argues about tool storage;
- asks about a village person who died/moved years ago;
- repairs something unimportant with Neris;
- admits a memory may simply be wrong;
- lets Neris change the workbench without resetting it.

## Neris / Orin

`ordinary irritation/love -> almost-confession -> loss -> idealized memory fragments -> accusation -> boundary -> future honesty`

Opening must contain enough warmth that Still-Cairn does not rely on “father” as an abstract label.

## Neris / Maelor

`suspicious stranger -> useful mentor -> personal connection -> discomfort -> betrayal -> ideological adversary -> tragic accountability`

Need at least two genuinely useful interactions where Maelor does not advance his hidden scheme directly.

---

# Intensity fail conditions

Flag screenplay drafts if:
- every dungeon ends with a solemn revelation;
- Ilyra and Neris discuss Mercy Window in three consecutive scenes;
- Tessa has no funny scene for more than ~2 major movements;
- the player cannot spend ten minutes in a settlement without hearing the central theme;
- every regional leader gives a speech after a boss;
- Unringing is presented only as checklists and war preparation;
- the finale becomes visually louder than emotionally clearer.
