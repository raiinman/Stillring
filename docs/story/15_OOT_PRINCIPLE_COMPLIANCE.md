# Project Stillring — Ocarina-of-Time Principle Compliance Audit

**Status:** canonical structural comparison  
**Reference:** `docs/reference/OOT_100_PERCENT_ANATOMY.md`

This document does **not** compare Stillring to Ocarina of Time by copying its story, characters, items, maps, dungeon layouts, melodies, scene choreography, or quest instructions.

It compares Stillring against the **functional adventure-design principles** already extracted from the 100% Ocarina walkthrough and Nintendo design commentary.

The goal is:

> preserve the clarity, cadence, interconnection, recontextualization, and memorable recurrence that make Ocarina flow — then go further by making Orra more socially alive and causally responsive.

---

# Principle 1 — Start small enough that the player owns home

## Ocarina structural lesson
Tutorial home is emotionally legible before the wider world opens.

## Stillring status
**PASS, with screenplay caution.**

Brindle contains:
- ordinary repair work;
- Orin relationship;
- Tessa spar;
- First Ring ritual;
- optional village interactions;
- visible revisit promises.

## Improvement locked
Protect 45–60 minutes of ordinary Brindle without turning every object into ominous foreshadowing.

---

# Principle 2 — Leaving home should expand scale, not dump lore

## Structural lesson
The first field/city transition makes the world feel larger and gives a clear political/social objective after basic competence.

## Stillring status
**PASS.**

Road -> Meridian ridge -> Cairnspire queue works well.

## Caution
Tessa's infrastructure explanation must remain attached to a broken road relay rather than becoming encyclopedia dialogue.

---

# Principle 3 — Give the player ownership of the wider world before repeated dungeon cadence

## Structural lesson
The early exploration intermission previews regions, optional systems, future dependencies, and side content before the plot accelerates.

## Stillring previous status
**PARTIAL / FAILED LATE-REGION OWNERSHIP.**

First Circuit previewed Rootmere, Saltreach, Emberstep but did not physically establish High Aerie, Mireglass, or Cairnfall before Grand Ring.

## Correction
Expanded First Circuit now includes short ordinary-life postcards in all six major regions.

This is one of the most important changes in the red-team pass.

---

# Principle 4 — New tools are verbs, not trophies

## Structural lesson
Major progression tools should solve immediate dungeon problems, affect combat/traversal, and reopen earlier spaces.

## Stillring status
**PASS.**

- Anchor Line: tension / traversal / component removal / revisits.
- Glasslung: pressure / water movement / combat interruption / revisits.
- Temper: thermal state / defense / material manipulation / revisits.
- Vane: authored airflow / fall control / projectile/pressure interaction / revisits.
- Mirror Nail: local state commitment / combat tangibility / traversal / revisits.
- seam mode: late state traversal / puzzle synthesis / old seams.

## Caution
Do not over-restrict authored targets so tools feel like color-coded locks.

---

# Principle 5 — World-state change should transform known geography

## Structural lesson
The changed-world pivot gets power from places, people, and routes the player already knows.

## Stillring previous status
**PARTIAL.**

Brindle / Cairnspire / first three regions worked. Later three regions did not.

## Correction
Six-region pre-pivot postcards + world-pulse ledger.

## Target
The Grand Ring montage should not introduce a single major normal-world location for the first time.

---

# Principle 6 — The first post-pivot movement should restore agency fast

## Structural lesson
After a large story reversal, give the player a satisfying new mobility/agency transformation rather than keeping them powerless for hours.

## Stillring status
**GOOD IDEA, UNDER-SEEDED.**

Line Skiff is strong mechanically but originally appears after Grand Ring without enough prior relationship.

## Correction
Seed retired skiff / old reinforcement strips / Tessa history during First Circuit.

Then changed-Brindle acquisition becomes payoff.

---

# Principle 7 — Setup now, payoff later

## Structural lesson
Actions in an earlier world state should produce later traversal/story consequences.

## Stillring status
**STRONG PASS.**

12 cross-state setup/payoff chains already exceed the baseline in causal specificity.

## Caution
Do not make one branch secretly superior. C01 is explicitly corrected so both repairs have different benefits/costs.

---

# Principle 8 — Optional systems should braid together

## Structural lesson
Traversal, combat, collectibles, upgrades, minigames, social sequences, and revisits should depend on each other rather than exist as isolated menus.

## Stillring status
**STRONG PASS.**

Examples:
- Line Skiff + Drift Knots + Roadhand Pulse + later tools;
- testimonies + Witness Interval;
- mastery trials improving core verbs;
- Unstandard Tool spanning specialists and travel constraints;
- Faults gated across the verb set;
- community projects using prior patterns/relationships.

## Red-team caution
There may be **too much completion inventory** for a 20–30 hour target if every entry demands bespoke narrative framing.

Compact systems (Faults, Shards, vaults) must remain compact.

---

# Principle 9 — Side quests should make NPC observation matter

## Structural lesson
The best social side content depends on understanding people and revisiting relationships.

## Stillring status
**PASS structurally, needs tonal rebalance.**

24 contracts have authored people and recurrence.

## Problem
Too many directly restate the central philosophical conflict.

## Correction
Add non-completion-bearing life interactions and classify side material as:
- theme-bearing;
- character-bearing;
- life-bearing.

Not every resident exists to debate infrastructure philosophy.

# Principle 10 — Major dungeons should vary cadence and scale

## Structural lesson
Not every major progression beat should be the same full-dungeon shape. Short interstitial spaces, perception detours, social gates, and large spatial machines vary rhythm.

## Stillring status
**PASS.**

- Root Cathedral: living load/water.
- Tide Foundry: pressure/water.
- Kiln of Names: thermal/material.
- High Aerie: two-part regional campaign.
- Bone Archive: compact interstitial research space.
- Palace Under Reeds: reflection/commitment.
- Fallen Orrery: reference-frame machine.
- Still-Cairn: civic dual-layer release dungeon.
- Null Meridian: synthesis.

## Caution
Boss moral grammar is currently less varied than dungeon grammar.

# Principle 11 — Perception verbs should first be taught, then exploited broadly

## Structural lesson
A new way of seeing the world should create a meaningful revisit wave and later become necessary in a larger challenge.

## Stillring status
**PASS.**

Palinode -> Bone Archive teaching -> Mireglass necessity -> old-region records -> final false histories.

Mirror Nail -> Mireglass -> broad echo-state revisit wave -> Cairnfall / Still-Cairn / Null synthesis.

## Stillring previous status
**PARTIAL.**

## Correction
First Circuit Graymile skiff seed + Tessa route history.

# Principle 13 — The route should alternate story pressure and optional freedom

## Structural lesson
Main beat -> optional sweep -> new verb -> revisit -> dungeon -> consequence -> sweep.

## Stillring status
**PASS IN DESIGN, NEEDS EMOTIONAL PROTECTION.**

The 100% route already braids sweeps after major verbs.

## Red-team correction
Do not foreground completion sweeps after:
- Emberstep three-point discovery;
- Grand Ring;
- full Mercy Window reveal;
- Orin confession.

Those moments need urgency or silence, not collectible cleanup prompts.

---

# Principle 14 — Player objective should be obvious even when mythology is not

## Structural lesson
The player can remain curious about the world while knowing what to do next.

## Stillring problem
Post-Ilyra sequence currently explains a complete three-relation plan immediately.

That makes objective clarity come from over-explanation.

## Correction
Give one immediate human/practical objective at a time:
- get out of Hush;
- find Tessa;
- reach High Aerie's moving relation;
- follow evidence from what that teaches;
- use Cairnfall to locate the missing release relation;
- find Orin.

The mythology can remain partially unknown.

# Principle 15 — The antagonist should be felt before the final fight

## Structural lesson
The antagonist's influence should shape the world and player route before confrontation.

## Stillring status
**PASS, but dialogue needs restraint.**

Maelor:
- protects the Key;
- gives useful diagnostics;
- shares Ilyra history;
- manipulates three references;
- shapes the opening damping failure;
- appears through system effects and records.

## Correction
Reduce explanatory appearances. His absence/systemic influence should carry weight.

---

# Principle 16 — Final dungeon is the curriculum exam

## Structural lesson
The finale recombines known verbs rather than adding a last-minute core language.

## Stillring status
**STRONG PASS.**

Null Meridian explicitly pairs old mechanics and reveals service architecture behind sacred presentation.

## Caution
Avoid one room per region or one color-coded trial per Countertone.

---

# Principle 17 — Final encounter should resolve what the player has been doing, not only what characters have been saying

## Structural lesson
The end should synthesize mechanic and story.

## Stillring status
**PASS.**

The final act is decommissioning the central heart using learned relations/verbs.

## Improvement
Listener alternatives should incorporate actual player memories from ordinary/optional life so the temptation is personal rather than generic.

---

# Principle 18 — End by returning emotional attention to ordinary life

## Structural lesson
A large mythic story becomes memorable when the ending restores human-scale places and relationships.

## Stillring status
**STRONG PASS.**

Brindle walk, imperfect bells, Tessa road, family rebuilding all fit.

## Caution
Credits cannot make every political argument look solved.

# Non-copy boundary

Adhering to these principles never authorizes:
- copying OoT quest order scene-for-scene;
- matching child/adult plot expression;
- sacred-object collection analogues;
- copied dungeon themes/layouts;
- familiar character-role renames;
- musical-key progression;
- copied NPC quest dependencies;
- map correspondence;
- item visual/functional clones;
- dialogue or cutscene reconstruction.

Stillring uses the **design jobs**, then solves them through Orra-specific infrastructure, characters, tools, communities, and themes.
