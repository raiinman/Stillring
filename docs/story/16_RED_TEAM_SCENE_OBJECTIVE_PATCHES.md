# 1. Opening causality / reveal timing

## Authorial truth

First Ring is both festival and annual full-reference maintenance check.

Maelor deliberately disables one emergency damping layer so an already-dangerous Meridian drift cannot be hidden for another year.

He expects a containable regional crisis.

The network releases more Hush pressure than predicted.

Ilyra's buried Cantor Key acts as a stubborn non-Meridian local reference and concentrates the Vale workshop seam.

Orin is taken by consequence, not targeting.

## Player reveal sequence

### Opening
Player knows none of this.

### After Rootmere
A maintenance comparison establishes the First Ring damping record is abnormal, but sabotage is not proven.

### Saltreach
Procurement/maintenance access shows high-level Meridian paths existed outside ordinary field crews.

### Emberstep
Three-point records + restricted data prove the damping failure could not have been an ordinary regional error.

### Grand Ring confrontation
Neris directly accuses Maelor of disabling damping.

Maelor admits the intervention and says he intended to expose the failure, not to take Brindle or Orin.

This is not an exoneration.

# 2. Why Neris / why the Cantor Key

## Authorial truth

Normal Bellwarden diagnostics assume Meridian as master reference.

After the central failure they self-correct toward the very system that is wrong.

Ilyra's prototype Key can compare local relationships without first accepting Meridian.

During the Brindle seam overload, the damaged Key partially writes Neris's emergency local calibration into field memory.

It remains workbench-usable by others, but sustained field use drifts unless Neris carries/adjusts it.

Retraining it safely would take longer than the crisis allows.

This is accident/circumstance, not prophecy or bloodline.

## Scene override — M02-S02

Maelor's intervention during attempted confiscation now includes a physical demonstration:
- Key drifts when Maelor carries it away from Neris;
- stabilizes when Neris takes it;
- Maelor says: **“For now, it remembers you better than it remembers its own instructions.”**

## Motivation effect

Maelor needs Neris to produce three clean stabilized crisis references.

Rusk has a valid safety reason to want the Key controlled.

# 15. 100% route principle override

A completion route remains a braid, but **narrative pressure outranks cleanup efficiency**.

The most efficient route is not automatically the canonical presentation if it would make Neris behave absurdly.

After urgent discoveries, completion opportunities can remain technically available without UI/story nudging the player toward them.

100% should feel like living through more of Orra, not Neris forgetting an emergency to vacuum icons.
