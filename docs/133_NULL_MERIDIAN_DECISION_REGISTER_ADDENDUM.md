# Governing player-facing idea

> **Meridian keeps forcing distinct local things toward one authoritative state. Neris progresses by allowing legitimate disagreement, preserving contradiction where useful, letting past states become past, and finally removing central command without destroying coordination.**

---

# Six sequence summaries

## S1 — Hanging Walk

Player sentence:

> **These walkways are being forced to move together, and they are tearing themselves apart. Let them move separately.**

Primary relationships:
- Anchor;
- Vane;
- established moving-structure/airflow language.

## S2 — Equal House

Player sentence:

> **Meridian is forcing three different machines to run the same way. Put each one back into the condition it actually needs.**

Primary relationships:
- Glasslung;
- Temper;
- heterogeneous safe operating states.

## S3 — The Door That Was There

Player sentence:

> **The official plans say there was never a door here. The worker records say there was. Find what happened and use the old doorway.**

Primary relationships:
- Palinode;
- Mirror;
- historical contradiction;
- local state commitment.

## S4 — Corrected Passage

Player sentence:

> **Meridian keeps choosing one version of the route and erasing the other. Hold the part you need, cross through the Hush, and keep moving.**

Primary relationships:
- Mirror;
- Hush seam mode;
- traversal;
- core combat pressure.

Objective is traversal, not mandatory extermination.

## S5 — Battle That Won't End

Player sentence:

> **Meridian is replaying an old fight on top of the one happening now. Find which formation is actually present, then fight it normally.**

Primary relationships:
- Cantor/Palinode-style diagnosis at room/formation level;
- preserved history;
- core lock-on combat.

Cantor does not expose enemy AI or future attacks.

## S6 — Take It Off the Bell

Player sentence:

> **These systems already know how to run without Meridian. Put them on their local controls, then shut the master relay off.**

Primary relationships:
- source-owned local service states;
- mandatory Common Measure;
- physical master-relay disconnection;
- visible independent continuation.

`pattern.local_accord` remains optional.

# Maelor / Listener boundary

Maelor and Listener are not counted among the six synthesis sequences.

Maelor:
- remains primarily a core-combat confrontation;
- escalates known Stillring relationships;
- may use preserved/false histories, delayed echoes, pressure/air, silence/redundant tells, and existing world-state interactions;
- may not become six inventory locks or a new boss-only control language.

Listener:
- is not a third health-bar boss;
- makes preserved desirable states playable/legible;
- demonstrates why a pleasant present that cannot become past also cannot become a future;
- ends through player-performed decommission/release of Meridian's central heart.
