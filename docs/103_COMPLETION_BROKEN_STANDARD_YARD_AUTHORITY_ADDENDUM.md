## Decision question

Does the Broken Standard Yard deserve its single completion-bearing mastery-complex slot, and how should its seven commissions operate so the Yard is a genuine synthesis capstone rather than nine Skill Trials stapled together, a boss rush, or a long no-checkpoint endurance test?

## Fresh research conclusion

**Keep the Broken Standard Yard and all 7 existing commissions.**

The Yard should count as **ONE completion-bearing mastery complex**, satisfied only after all seven distinct commissions have each earned their own internal qualifying clear.

The seven commissions are not seven extra global completion items. They are the internal anatomy of one optional synthesis capstone.

The decisive distinction is:
- Skill Trials ask, "Can you demonstrate this learned relationship?"
- Broken Standard Yard asks, "Can you adapt several learned relationships when the standard setup is wrong?"
- Null Meridian's final synthesis remains story-critical and narratively specific; the Yard is optional mastery practice/testing and must not duplicate the finale's exact sequence or stakes.

The governing principle is:

> **The Yard tests adaptation, not endurance.**

## Locked philosophy

> **Know the rule well enough to know when the setup makes the rule wrong.**

## Completion identity

**Semantic ID:** `mastery.broken_standard_yard`  
**Internal commissions:** 7  
**Global completion weight:** 1 mastery-complex item  
**Completion event:** all seven internal commission-clear facts settled, followed by the Yard's final recognition/reward transaction

The journal/workbench may display `5 / 7 commissions` for internal progress without exposing seven separate global percentage items.

## Availability / entry gates

The Yard becomes available in Unringing after all **critical-path major verbs** required by its baseline commission set have been learned.

It does **not** require:
- all 14 Tool Mastery Upgrades just to enter;
- all nine Skill Trials;
- all eight Optional Elites;
- the Freehand Frame;
- all Drift Knots;
- all Community Projects;
- arbitrary completion percentage.

Specific commissions may advertise one explicit optional mastery prerequisite when that capability is part of the authored test.

### Commission 6 — Seam Brace gate
Commission 6 currently uses seam mode + **Seam Brace**. Seam Brace is a locked optional Tool Mastery Upgrade. This is allowed under `docs/97` because advanced optional content may clearly require a specific mastery capability.

Rules:
- the Yard itself may be discovered/entered before Seam Brace ownership;
- Commission 6 clearly shows the missing prerequisite and owning acquisition route once discovered/known;
- other eligible commissions remain practiceable;
- Commission 6 cannot award the prerequisite it requires;
- no other commission silently assumes every optional upgrade.

This avoids turning "mastery complex" into "equip the completionist build."

## Seven-commission validation

### Commission 1 — Load Under Fire
**Systems:** Anchor Line + lock-on combat.  
**Problem:** preserve a suspended relief load while pressure arrives from two directions; killing everything first is invalid because the load keeps drifting.  
**Why it survives:** combat and load management compete for attention. This is synthesis, not a combat arena with a decorative crane.

### Commission 2 — Flooded Heatline
**Systems:** Glasslung + Temper + Breath Ledger.  
**Problem:** restore a heat exchanger in partial flood while two incompatible materials require different safe ranges.  
**Why it survives:** pressure, environment, and thermal state interact continuously; the solution is maintaining relationships, not executing one tool tutorial.

### Commission 3 — Silent Diagnosis
**Systems:** core combat + Palinode + visual reads.  
**Problem:** identify the physically committed threat among three apparent threats while audio tells are intentionally suppressed.  
**Why it survives:** it tests visual/state literacy under pressure, but accessibility authority requires equivalent non-audio information rather than treating deafness as the challenge.

### Commission 4 — Moving Relief
**Systems:** Vane + Anchor + movement.  
**Problem:** move a fragile medical rig across changing suspension infrastructure; dropping it changes the route rather than hard-failing immediately.  
**Why it survives:** recovery becomes part of mastery. A mistake creates a new problem instead of only restarting the room.

### Commission 5 — Contradictory Machine
**Systems:** Palinode + Mirror Nail.  
**Problem:** overlapping working historical configurations must inform a safe present state without deleting either record.  
**Why it survives:** the player synthesizes contradictory truth instead of selecting a designer-marked "correct" layer.

### Commission 6 — Close Behind You
**Systems:** seam mode + Seam Brace + Ending principle.  
**Problem:** leaving every seam open makes traversal easy but destabilizes the departure area; useful routes must be closed after commitment.  
**Why it survives:** this is a playable consequence/closure exam, not a traversal speed room.

### Commission 7 — The Field Commission
**Systems:** player-selected loadout; at least four major verbs.  
**Problem:** an under-specified failed civic machine presents symptoms and physical constraints rather than a quest-objective sequence. Multiple safe solutions qualify.  
**Why it survives:** this is the Yard's actual thesis. The verifier judges physical/system outcomes, not whether the player reproduced the designer's preferred sequence.

## Commission 7 multi-solution authority

The Field Commission must be authored as a **constraint verifier**, not a hidden solution script.

A qualifying solution must satisfy a reviewed set of outcome predicates such as:
- no remaining unsafe load above allowed tolerance;
- required civic function restored or safely bypassed;
- no prohibited persistent leak/heat/reference instability;
- protected people/critical equipment remain inside safe outcome bounds;
- any opened temporary route/seam is left in an allowed final state.

The verifier may record which tools/route were used for telemetry and testing, but it must not reject a physically valid solution merely because a different verb sequence was expected.

If production discovers that only one sequence actually works, Commission 7 has failed its design purpose and must be revised.

## Completion-versus-score semantics

Each commission has one internal **Qualifying Clear** based on its authored semantic outcome.

All seven qualifying clears are required once for `mastery.broken_standard_yard`.

Not required for 100%:
- par times;
- S/A/B ranks;
- no-damage clears;
- no-reset clears;
- one-session seven-commission streak;
- leaderboard placement;
- lowest tool count;
- "perfect" Field Commission solution;
- repeated clears.

After first discovery/attempt, commissions may be practiced individually. Optional personal-best/challenge metrics may exist but remain separate from Completion Ledger authority.

## No endurance-gauntlet authority

The Yard is **not** a seven-room resource attrition run.

Nintendo's Trial of the Sword is a useful contrast: it deliberately strips equipment and asks the player to survive sequential combat rooms as a gruelling endurance challenge. Stillring does not copy that structure because the Yard's thesis is adaptive systems understanding, not survival attrition. The reference informs the distinction, not the expression.

Locked rules:
- each commission has an independent entry/reset boundary;
- clearing a commission persists immediately;
- the player may leave the Yard and return later;
- no requirement to clear all seven in one sitting/life/resource pool;
- no between-room health/consumable attrition as the mastery test;
- no escalating enemy waves added just to make later commissions "harder";
- the final Field Commission is not preceded by a mandatory boss rush.

## Retry / checkpoint / resource isolation

Each commission is a development-quality practice environment in fiction.

Rules:
- failure/restart returns to the start or nearest meaningful internal checkpoint for that commission;
- retry setup is deterministic;
- intro explanations are skippable/reviewable after first viewing;
- commission-only consumables/materials are restored or virtualized;
- campaign inventory cannot be drained by repeated practice unless a global combat rule intentionally applies and a safe resupply path exists;
- a failed run never clears the commission or settles a reward;
- abandonment preserves previously cleared commissions;
- room-specific recoverable mistakes should be preserved when that recovery is the intended lesson, as in Commission 4.

Checkpoint placement must not split apart a relationship the commission is specifically testing. For example, if the point is maintaining two thermal states across a repair sequence, a checkpoint cannot simply start after the hard maintenance portion.

## Difficulty / accessibility authority

Any supported difficulty/accessibility configuration may produce a valid qualifying clear and the same Yard completion/reward.

The Yard may grade unrelated burden while preserving semantic outcomes:
- enemy damage/aggression;
- timing windows;
- camera/lock-on assistance;
- motion/shake;
- hold/toggle / repeated-input burden;
- objective recap;
- route/junction readability;
- state gauges and redundant sensory cues.

Current accessibility guidance supports grading difficulty components independently and keeping objectives reviewable. Stillring applies those principles without exposing hidden solutions.

### Silent Diagnosis accessibility rule
"Silent" means the **fictional station suppresses its ordinary combat audio tells**. It does not mean players who rely on sound are uniquely punished or players who cannot hear are expected to infer invisible information.

The physically committed threat must remain distinguishable through robust visual/state cues; optional haptic/accessibility cues may reinforce that information. Conversely, an accessibility setting that adds non-visual cueing does not invalidate the clear.

### Field Commission objective clarity
Commission 7 may be under-specified without being opaque. The player can review:
- observed symptoms;
- known physical constraints;
- protected objectives;
- currently measured unsafe states.

It does **not** list the required verb order or reveal a privileged solution.

## Reward — Open-Hand Coupler

The existing reward is **CONFIRMED**.

Effect:
- outside combat and active hazard states, Neris may swap one compatible optional tool modifier in the field instead of returning to a Refuge;
- no new mandatory verb;
- no raw damage bonus;
- no mid-combat loadout hot-swap;
- no bypass of tool compatibility or safe-state restrictions.

This is a completion/experimentation convenience reward, not critical-path power.

Mandatory story, Null Meridian, and baseline 100%-bearing content must remain solvable without obtaining the Coupler first.

The reward settles exactly once when the seventh required commission and Yard completion transaction settle.

## Relationship to Skill Trials

To avoid repetition:
- a Skill Trial may teach/test a narrower relationship;
- the Yard cannot simply reuse the same room with extra enemies;
- when the same verb appears, the **interaction conflict** must be new (e.g. load + combat simultaneously, contradictory history + present repair, movement + fragile relief recovery);
- layouts, failure states, and success predicates should be authored for synthesis rather than copied from Trial test beds.

If a Yard commission can be described as "Trial X, but harder/faster," revise it.

## Relationship to Null Meridian

The Yard is not a rehearsal of the story finale's exact puzzle sequence.

Allowed overlap:
- general learned principles;
- multi-system thinking;
- closure/commitment consequences;
- multiple valid physical responses where story authority permits.

Forbidden overlap:
- exact Null Meridian geometry;
- exact finale state sequence;
- spoilers encoded as "training";
- a Yard reward required to solve the finale;
- treating the optional Yard as prerequisite certification for story resolution.

## Persistence ownership

Mastery/World State owns internal commission and Yard completion facts.

Persist:
- Yard discovered/available state;
- seven stable commission semantic IDs and qualifying-clear facts;
- explicit optional prerequisite visibility/state where needed;
- commission-specific recoverable state only when save design supports mid-commission resume;
- Yard complete fact;
- Open-Hand Coupler reward-settled/idempotency fact;
- optional personal-best/replay metadata separately from completion.

Completion Ledger derives **one** `mastery.broken_standard_yard` item from all-seven-clear + final settlement.

Save/load during the seventh clear/final reward transaction must not produce 6/7 after a legitimate clear or duplicate the Coupler.

## Mastery / Combat / Traversal / Completion System IDE

The shared workbench must expose:
- Yard availability and rejected-entry reasons;
- seven commission IDs / clear states;
- current commission and phase;
- owning systems/verbs;
- canonical qualifying-clear predicates;
- current world/arena physical-state telemetry;
- enemy/aggression state where applicable;
- optional prerequisite facts (especially Seam Brace);
- active difficulty/accessibility modifiers;
- checkpoint/reset/resource-isolation state;
- Field Commission outcome-constraint verifier and accepted-solution telemetry;
- Yard 7/7 derivation;
- Open-Hand Coupler settlement/idempotency;
- Completion Ledger reconciliation.

Required fixtures:
1. Yard unavailable before all critical-path major verbs;
2. Yard available with optional mastery upgrades incomplete;
3. Commission 6 visibly blocked until Seam Brace while other commissions remain usable;
4. first qualifying clear for each commission;
5. failure/retry early/mid/late in each commission;
6. leave Yard after partial progress, save/load, return with exact clears preserved;
7. supported accessibility/difficulty clear earns identical commission fact;
8. Commission 4 dropped-rig recovery path still qualifying;
9. Commission 7 at least three materially different safe solution routes accepted by outcome predicates;
10. Commission 7 unsafe near-solution rejected with explicit physical reason;
11. 6/7 state does not satisfy global mastery item;
12. seventh clear settles Yard + Coupler exactly once;
13. repeat commission after completion changes no global completion/reward state;
14. finale remains completable on a save where Yard is untouched.

## Red-team / rejection conditions

Revise the Yard or a commission if:
- it is recycled Skill Trial geometry with extra enemies/tighter timing;
- difficulty comes mainly from longer health bars or resource attrition;
- all seven must be cleared in one uninterrupted run;
- a late failure discards already-cleared commissions;
- optional score/rank/perfection affects 100%;
- all mastery upgrades are required as a generic loadout gate;
- Commission 7 secretly accepts only one designer sequence;
- accessibility/difficulty options invalidate completion/reward;
- a commission becomes a boss-wave gauntlet unrelated to its system problem;
- the Coupler becomes required for story/finale progression;
- the Yard exposes/spoils the exact Null Meridian solution;
- save/load duplicates reward or loses a valid clear;
- content exists primarily because the game "needs a final challenge."

### Final locked statement

> **The Broken Standard Yard remains one completion-bearing mastery complex made of seven required internal commissions. It tests cross-system adaptation, not endurance: commissions clear/persist independently, retries are local and deterministic, optional score/perfection never affects 100%, and Commission 7 accepts multiple physically valid solutions through outcome predicates rather than a hidden script. The Seam Brace may explicitly gate Commission 6 without requiring every mastery upgrade. Accessibility/difficulty assists retain equal authority, the Open-Hand Coupler is optional convenience power, and the Yard cannot become a rehearsal or prerequisite for Null Meridian.**
