## Major-tool curriculum rule

Every major tool/capability must have all five of these before its owning region is considered design-complete:

1. **Immediate teaching use** inside the space where it is acquired.
2. **Combat or pressure interaction** that is not just bonus damage.
3. **Overworld use** outside the owning dungeon.
4. **Documented revisit wave** containing old places made newly useful.
5. **Late-game recombination** with at least one older or newer verb.

A tool that opens only its own dungeon doors is a keycard, not an adventure verb.

## Boss design rule

A boss or major pressure encounter should test the language taught by its owning region **plus** at least one universal skill such as spacing, target selection, movement, guard/evade timing, or situational awareness.

If the optimal strategy ignores the regional mechanic and simply repeats basic attacks for several minutes, redesign it.

Likewise, if the fight is only a bespoke puzzle that discards core combat entirely, ask whether it should be a puzzle encounter rather than a boss.

## Region completeness checklist

A production-ready major region requires:

- visual landmark and readable route identity;
- traversal identity;
- ambient sound identity;
- local social conflict that cannot be solved by killing the boss;
- at least 3 enemy archetypes or meaningful variants;
- at least one optional repair/relationship chain;
- at least one optional secret using an **earlier** tool;
- one authored pre-Stillness → post-Stillness setup/payoff where applicable;
- major dungeon/domain or explicitly scoped interlude;
- boss/pressure test appropriate to that scope;
- new tool/mechanic/narrative capability;
- explicit revisit manifest for the new verb;
- first-visit state;
- post-domain state;
- post-Stillness state;
- post-local-disconnection state where applicable;
- completion ledger entries from `docs/10_COMPLETION_MODEL.md`;
- performance pass;
- save/reload regression pass.

## Completion integration rule

The 100% route is authored **inside** the progression plan, not appended after the critical path.

Every region must identify, before graybox sign-off:

- which optional content is reachable on first visit;
- what becomes reachable after each relevant major verb;
- what changes after the Grand Ring;
- what Mirror Nail / Palinode perception reopens;
- what seam mode reopens;
- which optional choices affect the Unringing or credits montage.

A region with blank revisit waves is not content-complete.
