## First Circuit before-memory contract

Before Rootmere's full crisis, the player must personally experience at least one ordinary-life anchor in each major region:

- **Rootmere:** Lio's ferry + freely moving bell-oak.
- **Saltreach:** working fog signals + harbor meal/berth dispute.
- **Emberstep:** shift change + successful pour + maker culture.
- **High Aerie:** normal wind span + Wind Kitchen.
- **Mireglass:** Lantern Ferry + reflection game.
- **Cairnfall:** public observation night + deliberately flawed teaching scope.

Graymile must also seed the mothballed Line Skiff and Tessa's prior route history.

If production cuts threaten these anchors, preserve the emotional function even if individual scenes shrink.

## Major-tool curriculum rule

Every major tool/capability must have all five before its owning region is design-complete:

1. immediate teaching use;
2. combat/pressure interaction that is not bonus damage only;
3. overworld use;
4. documented revisit wave;
5. late-game recombination.

A tool that only opens its own dungeon doors is a keycard, not an adventure verb.

### Relation discovery rule

Motion, Memory, and Ending are **not** presented as an upfront collection set.

- High Aerie problem is solved first; then **Motion** is named.
- Mireglass problem is solved first; then **Memory** is named.
- Orin's workshop is released first; then **Ending** is named.

Only after Still-Cairn may the story naturally summarize all three as a set.

## Boss / pressure-test rule

A boss or major pressure encounter should test its regional language **plus** at least one universal skill such as spacing, target selection, movement, guard/evade timing, or situational awareness.

Boss narrative grammar must also vary:
- Mossjaw: ordinary animal;
- Mawhart: liberation encounter;
- Leviathan: current abuse stopped, accumulated damage still fatal;
- Cinder Regent: hazardous machine;
- Choir of Talons: predators exploiting changed conditions;
- Varo, Mirror Widow, Gravemoon, Orin, Maelor each have distinct later functions.

## Region completeness checklist

A production-ready major region requires:
- visual landmark and readable route identity;
- traversal identity;
- ambient sound identity;
- ordinary-life before-memory where applicable;
- recurring local cast and social space;
- local conflict not solved solely by killing the boss;
- at least 3 enemy archetypes or meaningful variants where combat scope supports it;
- at least one optional repair/relationship chain;
- life-bearing interactions with no required reward;
- at least one optional secret using an earlier tool;
- authored pre-/post-Grand-Ring state;
- setup/payoff where allocated;
- major dungeon/domain or explicitly scoped interlude;
- boss/pressure test appropriate to scope;
- new tool/mechanic/narrative capability;
- explicit revisit manifest;
- post-domain state;
- post-local-disconnection state where applicable;
- completion ledger entries from `docs/10_COMPLETION_MODEL.md`;
- world-pulse changes while Neris is absent;
- credits recurrence for named people where earned;
- performance and save/reload regression passes.

## Completion integration rule

The 100% route is authored **inside** progression, not appended afterward.

Before graybox sign-off each region must identify:
- first-visit optional content;
- Anchor/Glasslung/Temper revisit effects where relevant;
- post-Grand-Ring changes;
- Palinode/Mirror reopening;
- seam-mode reopening;
- Unringing role;
- credits consequences.

A region with blank revisit or recurrence states is not content-complete.
