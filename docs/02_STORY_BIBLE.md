## Canonical spine

Neris Vale, a seventeen-year-old apprentice bellwright from Brindle, inherits the unfinished Cantor Key after a silent catastrophe pulls Orin Vale into the Hush. Neris travels to Cairnspire and is manipulated by respected acoustician Maelor Renn into retuning six regional Waybells, believing this will rescue the missing.

Each region exposes another part of the lie behind Orra's ancient bell network. The Meridian Bell did not cure an invading evil; it imprisoned and compressed the Hush, an echo-layer of abandoned possibilities, along with its emergent consciousness, the Listener. Maelor, broken by the deaths of his wife and son, intends to end grief by freezing the continent in one permanent instant through a project called Stillring.

When the restored Waybells allow Maelor to trigger the Grand Ring, Cairnspire and then Orra enter Stillness. Neris escapes into the Hush and finds Ilyra Vale alive after eleven missing years. Together they identify three natural Deep Wounds where Neris can learn the Countertones of Memory, Motion, and Ending.

After recovering Orin and helping communities begin disconnecting themselves from the centralized network, Neris enters the Null Meridian. Maelor is defeated but opens the Listener's prison. Neris realizes the ancient bellwrights and Maelor made opposite versions of the same mistake: both tried to defeat time.

The final act is not to repair the sacred machine. Neris deliberately destroys the Meridian Bell's central heart. The Hush settles into many small natural seams, the Listener is released from forced singular existence, Maelor survives to face public trial, and Orra replaces centralized sacred infrastructure with imperfect local responsibility.

Neris eventually leaves Brindle as a traveling bellwright carrying a repaired but permanently cracked Cantor Key. The post-credits scene establishes that a different bell is ringing beneath an ocean far beyond Orra.
